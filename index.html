Listening, reading, mistake list and catch-up. Keep the full study routine.

Daily study structure

KKS days · Sunday & Monday

  • 15 min: revise yesterday's words
  • 1.5 hours: KKS class and notes
  • 20 min: notes / PDF
  • 40 min: 20 new words
  • 15 min: 5 sentences aloud

Other days

  • 20 min: yesterday's words + 3-days-ago review
  • 1 hour: main task
  • 30 min: written practice
  • 40 min: 20 new words
  • 20 min: read, listen, speak
  • 10 min: 5 sentences / words before sleep

Friday test: 30 word meanings, 10 noun articles, 10 grammar sentences. Week 9: one Goethe A1 sample test daily, including Hören, Lesen, Schreiben, Sprechen, form filling and a short email.
