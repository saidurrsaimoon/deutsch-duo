German A1 Progress

তোমার original 9-week study plan — routine পরিবর্তন করা হবে না।

Study period: 1 Nov–31 Dec 2026 · Book: Netzwerk Neu A1 · Daily target: 3 hours + 20 words.

Weekly Curriculum

Weekly schedule

Sunday

KKS class 1.5h + notes.

Monday

Next KKS video 1.5h + PDF topic.

Tuesday

Read and listen to the book chapter. Weeks 5 and 8: KKS class too.

Wednesday

Write textbook Übungen in your notebook.

Thursday

Make 15–20 sentences, speak aloud and listen.

Friday

Revise grammar and words + 30-minute test.

Saturday

Listening, reading, mistake list and catch-up. Keep the full study routine.

Daily study structure

KKS days · Sunday & Monday

  • 15 min: revise yesterday's words
  • 1.5 hours: KKS class and notes
  • 20 min: notes / PDF
  • 40 min: 20 new words
  • 15 min: 5 sentences aloud

Other days

  • 20 min: yesterday's words + 3-days-ago review
  • 1 hour: main task
  • 30 min: written practice
  • 40 min: 20 new words
  • 20 min: read, listen, speak
  • 10 min: 5 sentences / words before sleep

Friday test: 30 word meanings, 10 noun articles, 10 grammar sentences. Week 9: one Goethe A1 sample test daily, including Hören, Lesen, Schreiben, Sprechen, form filling and a short email.

Vocabulary Notebook

Nouns with article (e.g. der Tisch), Bengali/English meaning and one example sentence.
